Travel Tips

Follow these simple tips to make your journey safe, comfortable and enjoyable.

1. Carry Important Documents

  • Government ID
  • Travel tickets
  • Hotel booking details
  • Emergency contact information

2. Pack Essential Items

  • Comfortable clothes
  • Medicine and first-aid items
  • Mobile phone and charger
  • Toiletries
  • Travel bag

3. Stay Safe

  • Keep your valuables safe.
  • Avoid unknown or unsafe places.
  • Keep emergency numbers with you.
  • Follow local rules and instructions.

4. Respect Local Culture

  • Respect local traditions.
  • Dress appropriately at religious places.
  • Keep tourist places clean.
  • Respect local people and communities.

5. Save Emergency Numbers

Service Number
Police 112
Ambulance 108
Fire Service 101
