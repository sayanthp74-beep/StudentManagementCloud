Popular Destinations in India

India is a country with beautiful beaches, mountains, forests, historical monuments and cultural attractions.

1. Kerala

Kerala is famous for its backwaters, beaches, greenery and beautiful hill stations.

  • Alleppey
  • Munnar
  • Wayanad

2. Goa

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, nightlife, Portuguese architecture and seafood.

  • Baga Beach
  • Palolem Beach
  • Dudhsagar Falls

3. Kashmir

Kashmir is known for its mountains, valleys, lakes and beautiful natural scenery.

  • Srinagar
  • Dal Lake
  • Sonamarg

4. Rajasthan

Rajasthan is famous for its forts, palaces, deserts and rich cultural heritage.

  • Jaipur
  • Amer Fort
  • Jodhpur

5. Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is famous for ancient temples, beaches, cultural heritage and traditional food.

  • Madurai
  • Ooty
  • Kanyakumari
